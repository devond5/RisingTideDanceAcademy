Pre-Placement vs Level classes

Pre-Placement

These classes are for younger or new students where teachers have not had the opportunity to evaluate the students' current progression within a style. These classes allow teachers to observe and evaluate a dancers skills before recommending a more intensive leveled class for your student.

Leveled classes

These classes are based on teacher recommendation and assessment. This means that the students skills and progression within a style have been evaluated and assessed. This means that these classes at these skill level may include students of varying ages.
