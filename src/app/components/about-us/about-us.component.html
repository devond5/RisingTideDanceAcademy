Our Story

Dance belongs to everyone.

We were founded on a simple, non-negotiable principle: every single student deserves access to world-class dance training, regardless of their background or where they start. High-caliber instruction shouldn't be a privilege reserved for a select few—it is the standard we set for every dancer who walks through our doors.

Where Excellence Meets Inclusivity

Technique, artistry, and individuality are the core pillars of our training. We exist to build strong technical dancers, confident artists, and an open culture where students are celebrated for exactly who they are. We know that true talent isn't defined by financial status or the ability to commit to an elite competition schedule. Too often, traditional studio models prioritize profits or place the vast majority of their focus on company members, leaving recreational or high-potential students behind. We reject that framework entirely. We understand that real-life circumstances vary, and we refuse to let barriers stand in the way of potential. The next generation's Misty Copeland, Bob Fosse, Gene Kelly, or Alvin Ailey might be sitting in a beginner class, just waiting for access to proper technical training and world-class education. At Rising Tide, we make sure that door is always open.

Beyond the Studio.

While we take pride in technical excellence, our mission extends far beyond perfect technique. We train the whole person. Our goal is to mentor well-rounded, empathetic, and respectful human beings who use dance as a powerful medium to share their own unique stories. We aren't just rasing dancers; we are rasing humans.

Your Journey, Your Passion.

Whether your dream is a professional career in commercial dance or classical ballet, becoming a choreographer or educator, or simply discovering the joy of movement, your ambition is valid here. No matter which path you choose—or how far you want to take your passion—every dancer at Rising Tide receives the exact same level of dedication, respect, and encouragement.
